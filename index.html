💋 A Quiet Beginning 💋

They met in the quiet way some lives change—without fireworks, without warning.

Elena worked late at a small bookstore that smelled of paper and rain. One evening, just before closing, a man came in shaking water from his coat.

The night everything shifted, the city was wrapped in fog. Hands brushed, then held. When he touched her face, it felt like a question. When she leaned in, it was an answer. 🔥

Later, they lay close, city lights blinking through the window. It wasn’t just desire — it was choice, trust, and something dangerously beautiful. 💞
